ABOUT ME

I'm a web designer, content specialist and developer who does the whole job. I build static websites, I work on every major website platform, and I have deep experience with Drupal.

The person writing the code is also the person writing the words and designing the graphics. Nothing gets lost in a handoff, and you deal with one person from start to finish.

Having come of age during the late-1990s tech boom, I developed an early foundation in web technology. As digital tools, design frameworks, and user expectations have evolved over the years, I have continually expanded my technical skillset to match the fast-paced demands of modern web development.

I currently reside in the Midlands of South Carolina, with my husband, and 5 children (and my other kids, my fur babies).
